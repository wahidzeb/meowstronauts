About Meowstronauts

Meowstronauts is a side-scrolling space flyer starring two cats on rockets, Sunny and Shadow, lost in space. You drag to fly, your cat shoots by itself, and every one of the six planets ends with a big boss.

Collect fish stars, fill the Mega Meow meter, swap cats, buy upgrades in the Space Shop, and earn up to 18 stars. Two players can play together on one phone.

3 quick tips

  1. Drag from any empty spot. The rocket follows your movement, so your thumb never hides the action.
  2. Each planet has three stars: reach the planet, collect 60% of the fish stars, and take 2 hits or fewer.
  3. Turn your phone sideways for a wider view and you will see hazards much earlier.
